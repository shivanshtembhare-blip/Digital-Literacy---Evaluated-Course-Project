# Social Media Do's and Don'ts Checklist
# Do's (Responsible Habits)

.Audit your privacy settings: Regularly review who can see your profile and posts to ensure your personal life remains private.

.Build a professional brand: Share your projects, certifications, and academic milestones to catch the eye of future recruiters.

.Network with intent: Use platforms like LinkedIn to follow industry leaders and connect with alumni in your field.

.Think before you post: Pause and consider if you would be comfortable with a future employer or professor reading your content.

.Verify sources: Check the credibility of information or news before sharing it to avoid spreading misinformation.

# Don'ts (Mistakes to Avoid)

.Post offensive content: Avoid sharing discriminatory, disrespectful, or inappropriate language and imagery.

.Badmouth your university: Refrain from complaining publicly about professors, staff, or fellow students.

.Share sensitive information: Never post photos of your ID cards, hall tickets, or private contact details.

.Engage in "cyber-venting": Avoid posting heat-of-the-moment rants that could be perceived as unprofessional or immature.

.Ignore your digital footprint: Don't assume that "deleted" posts are gone forever; always assume everything online is permanent.
